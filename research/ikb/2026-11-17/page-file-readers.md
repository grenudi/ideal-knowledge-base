# Who reads IKB.md, and who checks it (2026-11-17)

Readers found by grep over idealkb, docs, ideal-agent: AGENTS.md (docs' own line, idealkb's generic Recording paragraph) and the record-the-work skill pointed to it; docs' Makefile `verify` refreshed it when ikb was installed. Nothing else reads it. CI does not check it (docs' CI does not install ikb, by the orchestration's wish: a failed download must not block landings). Every line of it is a copy of a record header (records/*.sql), which people can open on GitHub, or of what `ikb help RECORD` prints; it changed whenever a record's header or ikb's wording changed (idealkb 0.1 to 0.3: five regenerations in one day) and cost 9-13 KB on the read path as soon as a skill pointed at it.
Others: tad's own man page and completions are built in packaging (tad/generated/ is gitignored, tad's .gitignore line 7), man pages are built by cargo-deb/rpm packaging, docs sites are built in CI at publish time; committed generated files are kept only where the generated form is the product people open on GitHub (tad's rendered docs/*.md, checked by `make verify`).
