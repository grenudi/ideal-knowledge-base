# Summaries over accumulating records, and research kept aside - how others do it (2026-11-17)

The material behind research `summary-layer-world` and `research-aside-world` in this base. The rows hold the
finding; this folder is read only when the finding is questioned. Asked by the author on 2026-11-17: when one
subject gathers many records its answer must not grow without bound; research must be kept with its sources
but not load every agent's start. Every source was fetched on 2026-11-17 by a read-only researcher.

## The case measured

linux-phone-oco/docs, `tad q about --subject audio-mic --format csv`: 9.4 KB at 18 records, 15.9 KB at 26
(docs' research kb-navigation-remeasured, and again on 2026-11-17 for this research). Its weight is in five
rows over 1 KB: one decision (2.1 KB) and four research rows; eight session-log rows add 3 KB.

## Per source

**Cochrane living systematic reviews** - Guidance v1.0, December 2019
(https://community.cochrane.org/sites/default/files/uploads/inline-files/Transform/201912_LSR_Revised_Guidance.pdf);
Elliott et al., J Clin Epidemiol 2017;91:23-30 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28912002/).
Searching and republishing are separate: searched monthly, republished only when new evidence is likely to
change the conclusions, or on a schedule set in advance (every 4 months in the busiest pilot). Between
versions a "What's New" line says: searched on date X, N studies pending, conclusions stand. No numeric
threshold for an important change exists - proxies only (direction of effect, certainty, a new population or
harm). Authors write the update; editors approve deferring. Whether a review stays living is checked yearly.
A warning: more than half of published living reviews never publish an update
(https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12018299/).

**Wikipedia** - Summary style (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Summary_style): long sections are spun
off into their own articles, a summary left in their place with a `{{Main}}` link; new material goes to the
child first, then is summarised in the parent; `{{Sync}}` marks a parent summary that fell behind. Article
size (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Article_size): over 8,000 words of prose may need dividing, over
9,000 probably, over 15,000 almost certainly; under 6,000 length alone does not justify a split. The lead
(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Manual_of_Style/Lead_section) summarises the most important contents
and stands on its own; featured articles' leads are 250-400 words; citations may stay in the body.

**ADR supersession** - Nygard 2011
(https://www.cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions): numbered, never reused; a
reversed decision is kept, marked superseded, pointing at its replacement. adr-tools
(https://github.com/npryce/adr-tools): `adr new -s 9` links both ways. MADR 4.0 (https://adr.github.io/madr/):
`status: superseded by ADR-0123`, a date of last update. PEP 1 (https://peps.python.org/pep-0001/):
`Superseded-By:` with a matching `Replaces:`; a resolved PEP is a historical document. RFC 7322
(https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7322.html): `Obsoletes:` / `Updates:` in the header.

**ArtCraft and PhotoCraft (storytold)** - https://github.com/storytold/artcraft: CLAUDE.md is `@AGENTS.md`; a
shared house-rules block synced from a common repository and pinned by commit and checksum (as idealkb's
generic part is pinned); `plan/` is a git-ignored notebook, not rules; no research folder - dated evidence sits
beside a write-up (`docs/performance.md` summarises, `docs/performance/2026-09-26-before.json` holds the raw
measure). PhotoCraft's AGENTS.md
(https://github.com/storytold/photocraft/blob/main/AGENTS.md): a five-minute orientation table; ROADMAP.md a
one-page summary; status tables generated by a tool, not written.

**Gas Town and Beads (Yegge)** - https://github.com/steveyegge/beads: `bd admin compact` summarises closed
issues 30 days after closing, written by a small model in a fixed form (summary, key decisions, resolution),
kept only if shorter than the original, the original snapshotted and restorable; run when 10 or more are
eligible. https://github.com/gastownhall/gastown: work state in git-backed hooks and the Beads ledger;
`gt seance` lets a new agent question an earlier session's logs rather than read a stored summary.

**Claude Code skills** - https://code.claude.com/docs/en/skills: the description always in context, the body
loaded when used; keep SKILL.md under 500 lines and move reference material to files read only when needed.
Anthropic's context engineering
(https://www.anthropic.com/research/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents): lightweight identifiers kept,
content loaded just in time.

**The counter-finding already held** - ACE (https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04618): knowledge kept by rewriting one
summary erodes (context collapse) and drifts terse; itemised additions keep the detail.

## What others converge on

(A) A short dated summary on top, linking what it condenses (Cochrane's abstract and What's New, Wikipedia's
lead and Main, PhotoCraft's ROADMAP, a skill's description). Two speeds: a cheap "checked, N pending,
conclusions stand" every cycle; a full rewrite only when conclusions would change, on a size, age or batch
trigger. Whoever changes the detail renews the summary (Wikipedia, Cochrane's authors); a background model
where nobody is at hand (Beads) - only shorter, the original kept. The detail is never thrown away.

(B) Supersede, never delete: the old version stays, marked, out of the default reading (ADR, PEP, RFC).
Detail one link away (Main, a skill's files, ArtCraft's dated raw evidence). No common practice of a dated
`research/YYYY-MM-DD/` folder was found - not known yet; ArtCraft's dated evidence files are nearest.

Risks: summaries erode detail (ACE, Beads' fixed form); summaries go stale (`{{Sync}}`; living reviews that
never update); the threshold for renewal is not known anywhere (Cochrane says so).

## What idealkb takes, and what it leaves

Taken: the summary on top with its date (lead, abstract); renewal on a size trigger of what was recorded since
(Beads' batch, Wikipedia's size) shown by a query rather than typed (Cochrane's What's New, computed: practice
28); the records kept beneath, never replaced (ACE, Beads' snapshot); condensed records reached by id
(Main); supersession one way, read off the other way; material one link away, dated.
Left: a background model writing summaries on a schedule (no writer here knows the subject better than the
session that recorded into it); every summary version kept as a row (git keeps them; the records are the
history); typing both ends of a supersession (one end is read off); a separate table of research summaries (the
research row already is one).
