Taken: two tables (where it can go; what was sent), a forward-only state vocabulary mapped across systems (Patchwork new=sent, errata Reported=sent, Verified=accepted, Held=held, DEP-3 not-needed=not-needed), forwarded as an address, applied_upstream apart, supersedes for versions, from_work as the relation link, file for what prose or patches the row cannot hold. Left out: review comment threads (link the archive), Patchwork delegates and checks, per-person trailers, mirroring a remote tracker's status, a separate inflow machine, a signed-off flag (the person's word is a state, ready, not a flag an agent sets).
